Privacy policy

PokeFindHQ Comment Replies · Updated October 6, 2026

PokeFindHQ uses this automation on its own Facebook and Instagram accounts. When you leave a new top-level comment on one of our videos, it can send you our Pokémon Restock Group invite by private message and reply to your comment. This policy covers that automation.

Information we use

Meta provides account, comment and post identifiers, timestamps, and information needed to check that a comment belongs to one of our videos. Its notifications may also include your username, display name and comment text.

Our automation database stores the identifiers needed to process the comment, the outgoing response text, delivery status and message or reply receipts. It does not store your comment text, username or display name. We do not receive your Facebook or Instagram password.

Why we use it

We use this information to send the group invite, acknowledge the message in a comment reply, prevent duplicate sends and investigate delivery failures. This flow uses a fixed response; it does not send your comments to an AI model. We do not sell the information collected by this automation.

Where information goes

The sending automation and its database run on a computer managed by PokeFindHQ. Meta delivers comment notifications and processes our outgoing messages and replies. Cloudflare verifies incoming notifications and temporarily queues their routing identifiers until our computer receives them. Our queue does not retain comment text, usernames or display names.

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or contact forms added by PokeFindHQ. Hosting and platform providers may process technical information under their own policies: Meta, Cloudflare and GitHub.

If you follow the invite and join the group, your use of Discord is covered by Discord's privacy policy. Joining the group is separate from this comment-reply automation.

How long we keep records

Automation records are scheduled for deletion 30 days after we receive the event. Cleanup runs at startup and hourly while the service is running. If the service is offline, overdue records are removed when it next starts. These limits apply to our automation database, not to records held by Meta, Cloudflare, GitHub or Discord.

Pending records in our Cloudflare queue are deleted after transfer to our computer or when they reach 30 days. Queue expiry runs hourly. These limits cover the queue we operate, not technical records retained independently by Cloudflare.

Questions and deletion requests

Send a private message to @pokefindhq on Instagram. For deletion, include “Data deletion request” and a link to the relevant comment, or enough information to identify the account and comment. See how to request deletion for details.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the automation's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
