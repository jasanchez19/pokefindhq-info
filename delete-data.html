Delete your automation data

PokeFindHQ Comment Replies · Updated October 6, 2026

You can ask us to delete records held by our Facebook and Instagram comment-reply automation without leaving the restock group.

Send a request

  1. Open @pokefindhq on Instagram and send us a private message.
  2. Include “Data deletion request” and a link to the relevant comment. If you cannot find it, tell us which platform and account you used and approximately when you commented.
  3. We will use that information to locate your records. We may ask you to confirm the request from the account that posted the comment before deleting them.

Requests are handled by PokeFindHQ, not by an automated deletion bot. We will confirm when we have removed the matching automation records.

What deletion covers

Deletion covers matching account, comment and post identifiers in our local automation database and any matching pending records in our Cloudflare queue. It also covers the outgoing response, delivery status and receipts associated with the local records.

It does not delete comments or messages on Facebook or Instagram, technical records retained independently by our service providers, your Discord account or your group membership. You can manage those through the relevant platform.

New comments you leave after deletion may trigger the automation again and create new records. Existing automation records are otherwise scheduled for removal after 30 days, as explained in our privacy policy.
