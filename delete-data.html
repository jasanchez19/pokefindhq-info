Delete your automation data

PokeFindHQ Comment Replies · Updated October 6, 2026

You can ask us to delete records held by the PokeFindHQ or Aisle Snags Facebook and Instagram comment-reply automation without leaving the Pokémon Restock Group or Dealify.

Send a request

  1. Send a private message to the account you commented on: PokeFindHQ on Instagram, PokeFindHQ on Facebook, Aisle Snags on Instagram or Aisle Snags on Facebook.
  2. Include "Data deletion request" and a link to the relevant comment. If you cannot find it, tell us which platform and account you used and approximately when you commented.
  3. We will use that information to locate your records. We may ask you to confirm the request from the account that posted the comment before deleting them.

The account owner handles deletion requests manually and will confirm when the matching automation records have been removed.

What deletion covers

Deletion covers matching account, comment and post identifiers in our local automation database and any matching pending records in our Cloudflare queue. It also covers the outgoing response, delivery status and receipts associated with the local records, plus matching QA and diagnostic copies.

It does not delete comments or messages on Facebook or Instagram, technical records retained independently by our service providers, your Discord account or your group membership. You can manage those through the relevant platform.

Deletion also stops future automatic invites. We keep only a keyed, one-way opt-out identifier to honor that preference while the automation operates. A new comment does not restart invites; message the owner if you want to resume. You can stop invites without requesting deletion by replying STOP to a message. Existing automation records are otherwise scheduled for removal after 30 days, as explained in our privacy policy.
